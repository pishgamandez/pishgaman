چکیده پژوهش و مبانی کاربردی

Ardabil plain accounts for over 20% of Iranian commercial potato production. Key losses stem from uneven tuber sizing, hollow heart disease, and late blight fungal outbreaks. Fertigating chloride-free Tiger K High potassium gel combined with foliar Potassium Phosphite activates endogenous phytoalexins while sizing tubers above 150 grams.
