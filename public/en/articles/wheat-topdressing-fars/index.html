Wheat Topdressing Fertilization Protocols in Fars: Timing, Dosages & Yield Optimization

هیئت علمی و تدوین: Cereal Nutrition & Field Crops Division, Pishgaman Paydar Falat Nik | آخرین بروزرسانی: 2026-10-11

چکیده پژوهش و مبانی کاربردی

Fars province represents a top grain basket in Iran characterized by calcareous soils with pH values reaching 8.2. Nitrogen efficiency rarely exceeds 35% without organic stabilizing complexes. This paper evaluates the synergistic efficacy of Challenger powdered humate and Top Star multi-micronutrients during tillering and stem elongation stages.

1. Pedological Environment and Calcareous Challenges in Fars

High active lime locks soil phosphorus and micronutrients like zinc and manganese, while high diurnal spring temperatures induce premature moisture stress.

2. Split Fertigation Protocol with Challenger Humate

Blending urea with Challenger powdered potassium humate slows urea hydrolysis, minimizing ammonia gas volatilization and promoting early crown root anchoring.
