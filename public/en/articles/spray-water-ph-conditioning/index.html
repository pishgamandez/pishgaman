Crucial Role of Spray Tank Water Acidification & Hardness Conditioning in Preventing Alkaline Hydrolysis

هیئت علمی و تدوین: Agricultural Engineering Council, Pishgaman Paydar Falat Nik | آخرین بروزرسانی: 2026-10-11

چکیده پژوهش و مبانی کاربردی

Most agricultural deep-well irrigation waters exhibit pH values between 7.8 and 8.8 combined with elevated calcium and magnesium hardness. These parameters trigger instantaneous alkaline hydrolysis of pesticides and nutrient lockup. This paper outlines optimal tank-mix conditioning.

1. Alkaline Hydrolysis and Agrochemical Inactivation

The chemical half-life of organophosphates and synthetic pyrethroids collapses below 20 minutes at pH 8.0. Regulating spray water pH between 5.5 and 6.5 completely halts active ingredient degradation.

2. Hardness Interference in Foliar Nutrient Cuticular Penetration

Dissolved Ca2+ and Mg2+ cations bond with anionic phosphates and micronutrient ligands, forming insoluble salt crystals on leaf surfaces that cannot cross stomatal cuticles.

3. PH Best Conditioner Performance

At 50 to 100 mL per 1,000 Liters of water, Pishgaman PH Best buffers tank pH to optimal 5.8-6.2 and chelates hardness cations, ensuring maximum pesticide and foliar fertilizer efficiency.
