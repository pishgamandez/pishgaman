Absorption Kinetics of High Ortho-Ortho Fe-EDDHA Chelates in Alkaline Calcareous Soils (pH > 8.0)

هیئت علمی و تدوین: Plant Biochemistry Department, Pishgaman Paydar Falat Nik | آخرین بروزرسانی: 2026-10-11

چکیده پژوهش و مبانی کاربردی

Iron Deficiency Chlorosis (IDC) is the most widespread nutritional disorder across calcareous belts. High bicarbonate ions neutralize ferric reductase at the root plasma membrane. This research assesses thermodynamic stability of Red Full Fe-EDDHA in overcoming alkaline precipitation.

1. Why Iron Sulfate and EDTA Fail in Alkaline Soils

At pH above 7.5, Fe2+/Fe3+ ions from inorganic sulfates or EDTA ligands rapidly precipitate as insoluble ferric hydroxides. In calcareous soils, abundant Ca2+ ions displace iron from EDTA, rendering it bio-unavailable.

2. Thermodynamic Superiority of the Ortho-Ortho Isomer

The EDDHA chelating ligand features two stereoisomers: ortho-ortho (o-o) and ortho-para (o-p). The hexadentate bonds of the o-o isomer encase ferric iron within a protective molecular cage that withstands hydroxide attack even above pH 9.0.

3. Agronomic Efficacy of Red Full Iron

Pishgaman Red Full delivers verified high ortho-ortho ratios and 100% water solubility, restoring deep green foliage and chlorophyll biosynthesis within 4 to 7 days after fertigation.
