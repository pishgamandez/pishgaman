Calcium and Boron Synergy in Pectin Middle Lamella Stability, Blossom-End Rot Prevention & Shelf Life Extension

هیئت علمی و تدوین: Horticultural Sciences Division, Pishgaman Paydar Falat Nik | آخرین بروزرسانی: 2026-10-11

چکیده پژوهش و مبانی کاربردی

Blossom End Rot (BER) in tomatoes, peppers, melons, and bitter pit in apples stems from localized calcium deficiency at distal fruit tissues during rapid cell enlargement. Because Ca2+ moves strictly via transpiration-driven xylem streams, targeted foliar sprays co-formulated with boron are imperative.

1. Localized Calcium Partitioning Deficiency

Developing fruits possess minimal transpiration rates compared to leafy canopies. As xylem sap flows toward transpiring foliage, expanding distal fruit cells face catastrophic calcium deficits, triggering plasma membrane collapse.

2. Boron Cross-Linking in Cell Wall Pectin Networks

Boron cross-links rhamnogalacturonan-II dimers in cell wall pectin matrices, anchoring calcium into durable structural complexes that reinforce peel firmness.

3. Pishgaman Nutritional Regimen

Foliar application of Full Charger (chelated Zn & B) combined with calcium solutions at 10-14 day intervals ensures flawless fruit skin, eliminates blossom end rot, and maximizes post-harvest export grade.
