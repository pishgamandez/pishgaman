High-Potassium Fertigation Mechanics in Phloem Sugar Translocation, Fruit Sizing & Anti-Cracking Protection

هیئت علمی و تدوین: Fruit Tree Physiology & Potassium Fertigation Division, Pishgaman Paydar Falat Nik | آخرین بروزرسانی: 2026-10-11

چکیده پژوهش و مبانی کاربردی

Potassium is indispensable for turgor maintenance and activating over 60 enzymatic reactions involved in carbohydrate partitioning. In late vegetative and fruit bulking phases, sub-optimal K levels trigger premature ripening, hollow kernels, and catastrophic fruit cracking.

1. Phloem Loading and Osmotic Sugar Transport

K+ acts as the osmotic counter-ion driving mass flow of sucrose from source leaves to developing fruit sink tissues. Adequate potassium directly accelerates kernel weight accumulation and color development.

2. Prevention of Fruit Cracking and Physiological Splitting

Irregular irrigation intervals combined with calcium-potassium imbalances induce rapid internal flesh expansion beyond epidermal elasticity limits. K buffering sustains uniform cell turgidity.
