Soil Salinity Management and Advanced Sodium Leaching Kinetics in Arid Orchards & Open Fields

هیئت علمی و تدوین: R&D Department, Pishgaman Paydar Falat Nik Co. | آخرین بروزرسانی: 2026-10-11

چکیده پژوهش و مبانی کاربردی

Saline-sodic soil stress is the foremost physiological bottleneck across arid crop production. Excessive exchangeable sodium disperses clay aggregates, crippling hydraulic conductivity and root respiration. This paper presents biochemical mechanisms of poly-hydroxy carboxylic conditioners in facilitating deep sodium leaching.

1. Osmotic Drag and Cellular Toxicity

When soil saturation extract electrical conductivity exceeds crop thresholds, osmotic potential turns intensely negative. Roots suffer physiological drought despite standing water, while Na+ and Cl- bioaccumulation induces leaf margin necrosis and blocks potassium and calcium channels.

2. Structural Clay Dispersion and Compaction

Hydrated sodium ions possess extensive ionic radii that force clay platelet repulsion, causing hardpan formation, crusting, and severe hypoxia in the active root horizon.

3. Biochemical Displacement via Salt Stop Formulation

Salt Stop provides high-affinity soluble calcium accompanied by activating carboxylic acids. It aggressively exchanges Na+ from clay surfaces, allowing displaced sodium to leach safely beyond root depths with irrigation water.

4. Practical Grower Dosage Protocols

Applying 5 to 15 Liters per hectare of Salt Stop via fertigation during pre-bloom, nut-sizing, or post-harvest followed by an adequate leaching flush restores optimal hydraulic conductivity.
