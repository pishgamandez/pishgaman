Cotton Boll Retention & Yield Nutrition in Golestan with Top Star & Full Charger

هیئت علمی و تدوین: Cotton Agronomy & Plant Physiology Group, Pishgaman Paydar Falat Nik | آخرین بروزرسانی: 2026-10-11

چکیده پژوهش و مبانی کاربردی

Summer temperatures exceeding 38°C in Golestan cotton belts induce massive boll shedding due to pollen tube failure and carbon starvation. This paper presents a targeted foliar mitigation program using Full Charger (Boron & Zinc) coupled with free L-amino acids (Doping).

1. Hormonal and Environmental Triggers of Cotton Boll Abscission

Ethylene and ABA accumulation at the pedicel junction prompts premature boll drop under acute daytime heat.
