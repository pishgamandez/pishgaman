Synergistic Roles of Zinc (Zn) and Boron (B) in Pollen Tube Elongation, Fertilization & Flower Abscission Mitigation

هیئت علمی و تدوین: Horticulture Advisory Council, Pishgaman Paydar Falat Nik | آخرین بروزرسانی: 2026-10-11

چکیده پژوهش و مبانی کاربردی

Spring flower and fruitlet drop causes severe yield decline in deciduous stone fruit, pome, pistachio, and citrus orchards. Zinc and boron serve as catalytic cofactors in hormonal signaling and ovule fertilization. This paper highlights Full Charger in maximizing fruit set conversion.

1. Zinc in Tryptophan and Auxin Biosynthesis

Zinc serves as an essential cofactor for tryptophan synthesis, the direct precursor of indole-3-acetic acid (IAA). Zinc deficits delay bud burst, causing rosette shoots and stunted early leaf blades.

2. Boron in Pollen Tube Germination

Boron participates in pectin cell wall assembly and pollen tube germination. Inadequate boron induces premature tube collapse prior to ovary fertilization, producing blank or deformed fruits.

3. Full Charger Formulation Advantage

Pishgaman Full Charger delivers an exact balance of chelated Zn and B alongside amine nitrogen, ensuring rapid bud absorption during green tip and popcorn stages, elevating fruit set by up to 40%.
