Impact of Leonardite-Derived Humic & Fulvic Acids on Soil CEC and Rhizosphere Architecture

هیئت علمی و تدوین: Dr. S. Ebrahimi - Soil Biology Specialist | آخرین بروزرسانی: 2024-10-20

چکیده پژوهش و مبانی کاربردی

With organic matter dropping below 0.5% in arid arable soils, high-grade leonardite humic acid applications restore soil porosity and chelate essential nutrients against leaching.

1. Soil Organic Matter Depletion and Cation Exchange

Depleted soil organic matter directly lowers CEC, leading to high nutrient leaching losses. Humic substances supply dense carboxyl and phenolic functional groups that hold nutrients in the rhizosphere.

2. Auxin-like Priming of Lateral Root Systems

Humic molecules stimulate plasma membrane H+-ATPase activity, mimicking endogenous auxin signaling that drives lateral root elongation and fine root tip proliferation.
