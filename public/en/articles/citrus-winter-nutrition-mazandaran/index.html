Winter Citrus Nutrition in Mazandaran: Post-Harvest Recovery & Frost Hardening

هیئت علمی و تدوین: Citrus Sciences & Subtropical Horticulture Division, Pishgaman Paydar Falat Nik | آخرین بروزرسانی: 2026-10-11

چکیده پژوهش و مبانی کاربردی

Commercial citrus orchards along the Caspian littoral undergo severe mineral depletion post-harvest. Applying foliar Full Charger (Zinc & Boron) combined with Doping amino acids raises cellular solute osmolarity, decreasing freezing points, while Redful iron fertigation prevents spring chlorosis.

1. Physiological Necessity of Post-Harvest Citrus Fertilization

Evergreen citrus continues biochemical respiration during winter. Floral bud induction requires optimal zinc, boron, and iron balances.
