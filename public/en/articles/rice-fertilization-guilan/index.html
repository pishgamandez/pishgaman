Complete Rice Fertilization Program in Guilan: Rooting, Tillering & Grain Filling

هیئت علمی و تدوین: Rice Science & Wetland Research Group, Pishgaman Paydar Falat Nik | آخرین بروزرسانی: 2026-10-11

چکیده پژوهش و مبانی کاربردی

Wetland paddies in Guilan face anaerobic soil reduction states that impair initial seedling rooting. Jumper provides bio-available phosphorus and humates for rapid root flush, while Tiger K High strengthens culm mechanical strength against monsoon lodging.

1. Transplant Shock Dynamics in Guilan Paddy Soils

Cold water temperatures in May cause seedling mortality. Rapid adventitious rooting stimulated by Jumper secures tiller count and lodging tolerance.
