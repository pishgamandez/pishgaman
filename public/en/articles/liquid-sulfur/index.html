Agronomic Efficacy of Liquid Sulfur (Sulmax) in Rhizosphere Acidification, Nutrient Mobilization & Soil Pathogen Suppression

هیئت علمی و تدوین: Soil Science & Plant Pathology Group, Pishgaman Paydar Falat Nik | آخرین بروزرسانی: 2026-10-11

چکیده پژوهش و مبانی کاربردی

High carbonate and bicarbonate levels sustain soil pH above 8.0 across arid agricultural belts, severely locking essential macro and micro elements. Unlike insoluble granular sulfur, Sulmax micronized liquid sulfur disperses uniformly via drip systems to actively remediate rootzone alkalinity and suppress fungal pathogens.

1. Micro-Particle Dispersion vs Conventional Granular Sulfur

Conventional elemental sulfur particles resist hydration and require years for microbial oxidation. Sulmax liquid sulfur features sub-micron colloidal particles that disperse instantly through drip emitters, establishing rapid rhizosphere acidification.

2. Solubilization of Fixed Soil Phosphorus & Zinc

Mild local pH reduction dissolves insoluble calcium-phosphate complexes, freeing bioavailable ortho-phosphate and chelated zinc for immediate root uptake.

3. Fungicidal Shield Against Soil-Borne Fungal Spores

Sulmax emits active sulfur vapor that disrupts fungal respiratory chains in powdery mildew and Fusarium, while deterring soil-dwelling mites and phytophagous pests.
