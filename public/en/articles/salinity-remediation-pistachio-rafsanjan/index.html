Pistachio Soil Salinity Remediation in Rafsanjan & Kerman with Sulmax & Salt Stop

هیئت علمی و تدوین: Soil Science & Salinity Remediation Division, Pishgaman Paydar Falat Nik | آخرین بروزرسانی: 2026-10-11

چکیده پژوهش و مبانی کاربردی

Rafsanjan plain spans over 80,000 hectares of commercial pistachio orchards facing critical aquifer degradation and electrical conductivities exceeding 8,000 to 15,000 µS/cm. Exchangeable sodium percentage (ESP) causes soil dispersion, hypoxia, and foliar marginal chlorosis. This paper investigates calcium exchange kinetics via Salt Stop and rhizosphere acidification using Sulmax liquid sulfur.

1. Pedological and Climatic Profile of Central Iranian Pistachio Orchards

With annual evaporation exceeding 2,800 mm against less than 100 mm rainfall, intense evaporative concentration pulls soluble sodium and chloride salts toward root zones, impairing moisture acquisition and potassium uptake.

2. Diagnostic Symptoms of Sodium & Chloride Toxicity in Pistachio

V-shaped foliar necrosis, branch dieback, flower bud abscission, and elevated blankness percentages characterize salt-stressed trees.

3. Dual Remediation Protocol: Salt Stop & Sulmax Synergy

Salt Stop provides high-affinity activated calcium complexes that displace adsorbed sodium ions, facilitating downward drainage. Sulmax liquid sulfur oxidizes rapidly, lowering rhizosphere pH from 8.3 down to 7.3, solubilizing locked micronutrients.

سوالات متداول کشاورزان و باغداران

؟ Does Salt Stop treat well water salinity or soil salinity?

It directly eliminates soil sodium toxicity and structural compaction by displacing sodium with active calcium, allowing safe leaching below the root rhizosphere.

؟ Can Sulmax and Salt Stop be tank-mixed directly?

No, apply them in alternating irrigation events or via separate fertilizer injector lines for maximum chemical efficiency.
