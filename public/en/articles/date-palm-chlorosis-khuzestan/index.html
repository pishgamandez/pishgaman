Remediating Date Palm Foliar Chlorosis in Khuzestan with Redful & Full Charger

هیئت علمی و تدوین: Date Palm & Arid Soils Division, Pishgaman Paydar Falat Nik | آخرین بروزرسانی: 2026-10-11

چکیده پژوهش و مبانی کاربردی

Date palm groves across Shadegan and Ahvaz encounter soil pH above 8.3 and severe summer temperatures above 50°C. Standard iron fertilizers precipitate rapidly. Applying Redful high-purity ortho-ortho Fe-EDDHA restores photosynthetic leaf area and cluster retention.

1. Soil Alkalinity and Iron Locking in Khuzestan Alluvial Soils

Karun river basin sediments contain elevated calcium carbonate that locks native iron, prompting rapid interveinal chlorosis on young fronds.

سوالات متداول کشاورزان و باغداران

؟ Why does standard iron sulfate fail in Khuzestan?

Iron sulfate precipitates instantly as insoluble ferric hydroxide in soils with pH above 7.5; only ortho-ortho Fe-EDDHA remains bioavailable.
