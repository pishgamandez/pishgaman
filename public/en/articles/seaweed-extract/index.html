Physiological Mode of Action of Ascophyllum Nodosum Seaweed Extracts in Fruit Cell Division and Biomass Yield

هیئت علمی و تدوین: Biotechnology & Plant Science Division, Pishgaman Paydar Falat Nik | آخرین بروزرسانی: 2026-10-11

چکیده پژوهش و مبانی کاربردی

Ascophyllum nodosum cold-extracted seaweed extract represents one of the most effective natural biostimulants globally. The rich profile of natural cytokinins, auxins, and alginate oligosaccharides directly stimulates cell division phases and boosts harvest weight up to 35%.

1. Cytokinin Elevation and Rapid Fruit Cell Division

In post-pollination fruitlet stages, the total cell count established during the initial 3-4 weeks dictates ultimate fruit size potential. Natural cytokinins in Fiction stimulate mitosis, resulting in greater cell density and structural resilience.

2. Alginates & Mannitol as Endogenous Chelating Ligands

Alginic acid complexes micronutrient cations into organically bound forms, dramatically enhancing foliar translocation and mitigating nutrient fixation in calcareous conditions.

3. Agronomic Application of Fiction Formulation

Pishgaman Fiction is processed via low-temperature cold extraction to preserve bioactive peptides and hormonal integrity, providing unparalleled frost resilience and enhanced fruit set.
