Managing Vegetable Salinity Stress in Isfahan: EC Remediation & Blossom-End Rot Control

هیئت علمی و تدوین: Protected Cultivation & Horticultural Sciences Group, Pishgaman Paydar Falat Nik | آخرین بروزرسانی: 2026-10-11

چکیده پژوهش و مبانی کاربردی

Vegetable growers across Isfahan face groundwater conductivities exceeding 5,000 µS/cm. Sodium competes directly with root calcium uptake, causing rampant blossom-end rot. Salt Stop provides active calcium displacement of adsorbed sodium while Sulmax remediates rhizosphere alkalinity.

1. Osmotic and Toxic Salinity Stress in Solanaceous and Cucurbit Crops

Excess sodium disrupts cellular potassium-to-sodium balances, reducing fruit flesh firming and inducing physiological wilt.

سوالات متداول کشاورزان و باغداران

؟ How does high salinity cause blossom-end rot?

Sodium competitively inhibits calcium uptake; calcium deficiency in rapidly expanding distal fruit cells causes cellular collapse and black rot.
